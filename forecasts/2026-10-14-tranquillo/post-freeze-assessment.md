# Post-freeze assessment of the Tranquillo forecasts

**Written:** 2026-10-14T10:47:19.496243+00:00 (after all four forecasts were frozen; the sealed files are unchanged).
**Purpose:** record known weaknesses *before* any results are seen, so the forecasts are judged fairly.

1. **Week-52 forecasts are extrapolations with a documented upward bias.** Ritlecitinib has no controlled data beyond week 24 in the corpus, and placebo/vehicle data end at week 24. The only check of week-24→52 extrapolation (topical ruxolitinib, TRuE-V) over-predicted by about +20 to +23 points (F-VASI) and +10 to +14 points (T-VASI). The F-VASI time-extrapolation fit also had one divergent transition. Expect the F-VASI75 week-52 forecast to err high, and treat its very wide interval (95%: 3.9–96.3%) as uninformative about magnitude. The informative part is the direction (P(active > placebo) ≈ 0.99).
2. **The T-VASI50 forecasts show no benefit over placebo**, at both week 24 and week 52. This follows directly from the data: in Phase 2b at week 24, ritlecitinib 50 mg reached T-VASI50 in 4.6% vs 9.1% on placebo. The model multiplies the week-24 effect by a time curve, so it **cannot represent a late-onset effect**, which is clinically plausible for total-body repigmentation. Phase 2b posted efficacy only to week 24 (the extension posted safety only), so the data that likely informed Pfizer's choice of T-VASI50 at week 52 as a co-primary endpoint are absent. **Low confidence; a negative result here would say more about the model than about the drug.**
3. **New-trial baseline.** Forecasts draw Tranquillo's placebo response from the distribution across all controlled trials, mostly topical-ruxolitinib populations. If Tranquillo resembles the oral Phase 2b population (F-VASI75 placebo 0% at week 24), the week-24 F-VASI75 forecast (median 16.6%) is likely high relative to Phase 2b's 7.7% for 50 mg.
4. **Note wording.** The sealed records' extrapolation note says "class-wide time course". v0.2 actually uses a route-specific (oral) time course. The wording was fixed in code after freezing; the forecasts themselves are unaffected.

**What would make the next version better:** extract results from publications (the Phase 2b extension; Phase 3 papers); add placebo data beyond week 24; give each instrument (F-VASI and T-VASI) its own time-course shape that allows delayed onset; and resolve how TRuE-V's week-52 extension results were analysed (completers vs imputation).
