Vitiligo Initiative

Evidence Engine — open, AI-native research for stopping spread and driving repigmentation.

Semantic search over the corpus

Search by meaning, not keywords. Returns the most relevant papers from PubMed and PMC Open Access.

Ask the corpus

Ask a research question. Answers cite the retrieved papers inline. Requires an Anthropic API key configured server-side.

Generate ranked candidates

From a research intent, the engine retrieves and asks the LLM to extract ranked therapeutic candidates with mechanistic rationale, evidence strength, and citations.

Clinical trials

Structured search over registered vitiligo trials. Sources: ClinicalTrials.gov (US NIH) and EU CTR (CTIS, EMA). WHO ICTRP next.
