Evidence Engine — Privacy Policy (draft)

Last updated: May 2026 · Draft v0.1 — not legal advice

Overview

The Vitiligo Initiative Evidence Engine (“the Service”) is a research and education tool. It searches public scientific literature and clinical trial registries. It is operated by the Vitiligo Initiative (legal entity to be finalized).

What we collect

  • Queries you submit — search text, questions, and hypothesis intents sent to Ask and Hypothesize endpoints.
  • Technical logs — IP address, user agent, timestamps, and error diagnostics for security and abuse prevention.
  • No account required — we do not ask for name, email, or health records to use the public tool.

What we do not collect

  • We do not require patient identifiers or medical records.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use the Service for targeted advertising.

Third-party processing

Ask and Hypothesize send your query text and retrieved public excerpts to a large language model provider (Anthropic) to generate cited answers. Do not submit information you would not want processed by a third-party AI vendor. Hosting uses cloud infrastructure in regions chosen by the operator (EU-first when deployed in Europe).

Retention

Server logs are retained for a limited period for security and debugging, then deleted or aggregated. Query logging policy will be tightened before any wide public launch; we aim to minimize retention of free-text health-related queries.

Your rights (EU / UK)

If you are in the EU or UK, you may have rights to access, rectify, or delete personal data we hold about you. Contact us at the address below. We will update this policy when a legal entity and DPO/contact are appointed.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions or data requests: privacy@vitiligo-initiative.org (placeholder — update before production launch).
