## Hypothesis

**Once-daily oral upadacitinib produces clinically meaningful repigmentation in adults with extensive NSV who have failed or cannot access adequate topical JAK therapy**, with manageable safety monitoring per rheumatology/dermatology JAK protocols.

## Why now

- Phase 2 RCT published (2024): dose-ranging upadacitinib vs placebo in extensive NSV.
- **Phase 3 programs active** (EU CTR 2023-506195-27-00; NCT06118411).
- Engine ranks upadacitinib **strong** across priors, graph, trials, and literature.
