# Stopping spread: what is measured, and what is known

Outcome 1 of [what success means](../success.md): no new or growing lesions in patients whose disease is active. This page maps the evidence (2026-10-23).

## Trials rarely measure it

**Registered outcomes.** Of 320 vitiligo trials in the ClinicalTrials.gov corpus, **15 interventional trials measure spread or disease activity directly**. That counts stabilization, arrest, new lesions, progression, VIDA, VSAS and VETF spreading; extent-only scores and observational studies are left out.
- **Posted results: 2, both score changes.** Both are ruxolitinib cream trials reporting VETF spreading or progression scores: the Incyte phase 2 (NCT03099304) and a Tufts pilot of 11 patients (NCT02809976).
- **None posted the share of patients whose disease stopped spreading.**

**No common definition.** Measures in use include:
- disease activity reported by the patient (VIDA);
- clinical signs of activity (VSAS: confetti-like lesions, trichrome, Koebner phenomenon);
- the extent of involvement (VES);
- "no new lesions in the past 30 days".

## What is known

| Evidence | Design | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Ritlecitinib 50 mg vs placebo, Tranquillo (EADV 2026 late-breaker LB-161, as reported by [HCPLive](https://www.hcplive.com/view/ritlecitinib-stabilizes-vitiligo-tranquillo-trial-iltefat-hamzavi-md), 2026-10-05) | Phase 3, randomized; active **and** stable disease enrolled | "Roughly 80% of patients receiving ritlecitinib remained stable compared with about 60% with placebo." Definition and timepoint not public |
| Dexamethasone oral mini-pulse (J Cutan Med Surg 2013, PMID 23815959) | Retrospective, 444 patients with progressive disease, no control | Activity arrested in 91.8% after a mean of 13 weeks; 12% relapsed in activity during follow-up |
| Methotrexate 10 mg weekly vs dexamethasone mini-pulse (Dermatology 2015, PMID 26278124) | Randomized, open-label, 52 patients | New lesions over 24 weeks in 6 of 25 vs 7 of 25; no difference |
| Mycophenolate vs dexamethasone mini-pulse (Arch Dermatol Res 2021, PMID 32737577) | Randomized, investigator-blinded, 50 patients with active disease | Disease activity fell in both by day 90, then **rose again after stopping** |
| Tofacitinib vs corticosteroid mini-pulse (J Am Acad Dermatol 2026, PMID 42628800) | Randomized, assessor-blinded, 24 weeks | No abstract available |

**What this shows:**
- **There is no placebo-controlled trial in active disease with published results.** Mini-pulse steroids are the standard, and are compared only with other active drugs.
- **In mixed populations, most placebo patients stay stable** (about 60% in Tranquillo). Measuring arrest needs patients whose disease is active at entry.
- **Arrest fades when treatment stops** (mycophenolate), as repigmentation does (durability, [outcome 3](../durability/README.md)).

## What is coming

Ritlecitinib's other phase 3 trials register "proportion of participants achieving disease stabilization": Tranquillo 2 ([NCT06072183](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06072183), to week 104) and Tranquillo LTE ([NCT06163326](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06163326)). Neither has posted results.

Not about spread, despite its name: the deucravacitinib trial's primary endpoint "rate of depigmentation" ([NCT06327321](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06327321)) is 50% improvement of facial depigmentation, a repigmentation measure.

## Gaps

- **A shared, patient-relevant definition**, for example no new or enlarging lesions over 12 months, with VSAS at entry to confirm activity.
- **A placebo- or vehicle-controlled trial in active disease**, and a check on whether the arrest lasts after stopping.
