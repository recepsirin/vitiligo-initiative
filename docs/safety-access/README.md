# Safe and reachable: long-term safety and access

Outcome 4 of [what success means](../success.md): acceptable for years of use, and accessible. This page maps the evidence (2026-10-23).

## Safety

| Evidence | Design | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Ruxolitinib cream, integrated phase 3 safety ([Dermatol Ther 2025, PMC12619853](https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC12619853)) | TRuE-V1, TRuE-V2 and the extension: 637 patients over 104 weeks, 867.9 person-years | No serious treatment-related adverse events. Malignancies, serious infections and thromboembolic events were rare (0.7, 0.5 and 0.2 per 100 person-years), none considered treatment-related; no major cardiovascular events or deaths. Most common: nasopharyngitis (7.2%) and application-site acne (6.0%) |
| JAK inhibitors in skin diseases, meta-analysis (JAMA Dermatol 2024, PMID 37910098) | 35 phase 3 randomized trials, 20,651 patients, mean age 38.5 | No significant difference vs comparators in major cardiovascular events and death (OR 0.83, 95% CI 0.44–1.57) or venous thromboembolism (OR 0.52, 0.26–1.04). **Mean follow-up 4.9 months** |

- **The labels are stricter than the evidence in skin disease.** The US FDA's boxed warning on oral and topical JAK inhibitors (major cardiovascular events, thrombosis, serious infections, malignancy, death) came from a trial in rheumatoid arthritis (ORAL Surveillance), in patients aged 50 or over with at least one cardiovascular risk factor.
- **Years of use are not covered.** The meta-analysis followed patients for months. The cream has two years of data. Oral JAK inhibitors have no multi-year data in vitiligo.
- **Stopping is also a safety decision.** Every year of continued treatment adds exposure no trial has measured. That is one more reason the stop-or-step-down question ([durability](../durability/README.md)) matters.

## Access

| | Ruxolitinib 1.5% cream (Opzelura) |
|---|---|
| **US** | Approved 2022. Cash price about $2,100–2,800 per 60 g tube (wholesale about $2,094), less with insurance or manufacturer programs ([GoodRx](https://www.goodrx.com/opzelura/how-much-is-opzelura-cream-without-insurance), [Drugs.com](https://www.drugs.com/price-guide/opzelura)) |
| **England (NICE [TA1140](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ta1140), 17 March 2026)** | Recommended for non-segmental vitiligo **with facial involvement**, from age 12, **only if first-line topical treatments have not worked or are not suitable**, with a confidential discount. List price £657 per 100 g tube. A draft in January 2024 did not recommend it; the route ran through an appeal, TA1088 (August 2025) and a rapid review |

**Oral JAK inhibitors are not approved for vitiligo.** Filings are planned: Incyte expects to file povorcitinib in the first half of 2027 ([Healio](https://www.healio.com/news/dermatology/20260505/topline-results-povorcitinib-yields-vitiligo-repigmentation-up-to-1-year)).

## Gaps

- **Multi-year safety of oral JAK inhibitors** in vitiligo's younger, healthier population.
- **Cost-effectiveness of maintenance strategies**, such as a cheap twice-weekly ointment instead of daily cream ([trial concept](../durability/maintenance-trial-concept.md)).
- **Access outside the US and Europe:** not yet mapped.
